# ADR-0003: BFF Is Out Of Scope For Now

## Status

Accepted

## Context

A backend-for-frontend could demonstrate full-stack architecture, but FeitozaUI is currently focused on UI platform maturity.

## Decision

BFF work will remain out of scope until the core library, Storybook, tests, public API, and showcase are stable.

## Consequences

- The showcase will use local or mocked frontend data first.
- BFF ideas may be documented in the future roadmap.
- The project avoids unnecessary backend complexity during the design system foundation phase.
