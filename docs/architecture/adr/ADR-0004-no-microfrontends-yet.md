# ADR-0004: Micro-Frontends Are Out Of Scope For Now

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Micro-frontends can demonstrate large-scale frontend architecture, but they add operational and architectural complexity.

## Decision

Micro-frontends will not be implemented until the core platform has a stable package, documentation, tests, showcase, and clear reason to split applications.

## Consequences

- The project remains focused on component platform quality first.
- Future micro-frontend ideas can be documented without becoming current scope.
- The roadmap avoids overengineering before there is a product need.
